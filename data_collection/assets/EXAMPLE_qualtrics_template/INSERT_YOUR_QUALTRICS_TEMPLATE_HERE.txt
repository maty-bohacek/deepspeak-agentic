Do not commit real Qualtrics exports to the public repo.

1. In Qualtrics: export your survey as a .qsf file.
2. Copy that .qsf into:
   data_collection/output/1.5_survey_templates/inputs/

Set QUALTRICS_TEMPLATE_BLOCK_ID in data_collection/config.yaml to your template block's "ID" from the QSF.
